02

Enterprise process improvement

Rethinking first-day account access

University of Arkansas System Division of Agriculture

Proposal presented to the CIO

I designed and presented a Microsoft Temporary Access Pass onboarding proposal to reduce manual helpdesk involvement and support a more self-service initial access process.

The opportunity & proposal

The operational need. Initial account access creates manual work for the helpdesk and friction in the onboarding process.

The proposal. Use Microsoft Temporary Access Pass as part of a more automated onboarding approach, connecting an identity capability to an everyday business process.

Current evidence. The proposal was presented to the CIO. Implementation and measured outcomes are not claimed here.

03

Research workflows & applied AI

Luther: research around the researcher

Personal project · Medieval history research

Research tool built

I built a research tool around a doctoral researcher’s workflow to aggregate medieval-history journal content, summarize articles and issues, and surface potential research gaps.

The design focus

Start with the workflow. Organize the tool around how a researcher reviews journal content and identifies questions worth exploring.

Support exploration. Bring aggregation, summarization, and potential gaps together to help the researcher navigate material and develop further lines of inquiry.

04

Systems design & automation

Vera: local-first personal automation

Personal project · Self-hosted infrastructure

In design & development

I’m designing a personal automation platform that connects local infrastructure, service integrations, and workflows, with explicit human approval for consequential external actions.

The architecture focus

System ownership. Center the platform on self-hosted infrastructure and deliberate integration between services.

Human oversight. Make approval for consequential external actions part of the design rather than an afterthought.

Current stage. This is an evolving personal project, not a completed production platform.
